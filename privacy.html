Privacy Policy — Quantum Wealth Studio

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Quantum Wealth Studio accesses only the minimum authorization credentials and account identifiers provided via TikTok OAuth 2.0 (such as access tokens, refresh tokens, and authorized scopes) required to upload and publish video content on behalf of the connected account.

2. How We Use Information

Collected authorization tokens and media metadata are used strictly to authenticate API requests with TikTok, upload and publish approved short-form videos, and monitor publishing status.

3. Data Storage and Security

Credentials and tokens are stored securely in restricted server environment variables and are never sold, rented, or shared with unauthorized third parties.

4. Data Retention and Revocation

You may revoke Quantum Wealth Studio's access at any time through your TikTok account security settings. Upon revocation or request, stored tokens are deleted from our system.
